A meeting assistant that takes the notes for you

Design reviews and project meetings move fast, and the decisions made in them are easy to lose. This Copilot agent sits in on Teams meetings in the background, follows the conversation, and turns it into a short summary with the decisions, action items, and open questions pulled out — so nobody has to choose between participating and taking notes.

How it works

Four stages, from a live meeting to notes in the team's channel.

01

Listen

The agent runs alongside the Teams meeting and picks up the transcript as people speak.

02

Segment

Split the transcript into speaker turns and topics so related discussion stays together.

03

Extract

Flag the moments that matter — decisions made, tasks assigned, and questions left open.

04

Summarize

Write a short recap with owners and due dates, and post it where the team will see it.

Under the hood

A simplified view of the pipeline — pull the transcript, flag what matters, then condense it into notes people will actually read.

meeting_agentillustrative

transcript = get_meeting_transcript(meeting_id)  # Teams transcript
segments   = split_by_topic(transcript)

for seg in segments:
    notes.extend(extract(seg, labels=["decision", "action", "open question"]))

summary = summarize(notes, max_words=150)    # short recap up top
post_to_channel(summary, notes, mention=owners(notes))
